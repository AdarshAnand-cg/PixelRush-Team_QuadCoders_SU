Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common hostel tiffin delivery and meal management questions.

How do I pause my tiffin during exams or vacation?

Meal Pause

You can pause meals anytime directly from your "My Subscription" tab. For lunch, toggle pause before 8:00 AM; for dinner, before 3:00 PM. Unused meal credits never expire and roll over automatically to your next month.

Where is my thermal dabba dropped off at the hostel?

Hostel Gate

Deliveries are handed over to your hostel's central security desk or reception with your name and room tag (#MM-SUB-49521). You will receive an SMS and WhatsApp notification the moment it arrives.

When and how is my ₹300 Dabba Deposit refunded?

Deposit Refund

The ₹300 insulated stainless steel container deposit is 100% refundable. Whenever you conclude or pause your subscription, our delivery partner collects the clean dabba and refunds ₹300 directly via UPI within 2 hours.

Can I customize my spice level or request No Onion / Garlic?

Customization

Yes, absolutely! We offer pure Jain preparation, low-sodium mild spice, and 100% pure vegetarian meal tracks at ₹0 extra fee. Update your kitchen preferences in settings anytime.
