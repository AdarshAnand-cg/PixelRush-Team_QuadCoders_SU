CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about confidentiality, kitchen quality, and support procedures.

❓

Who can request Student Meal Support?

Any enrolled student residing in hostel rooms, PGs, or rented apartments within our delivery radius experiencing temporary monetary hardship, delayed student remittances, or financial friction is welcome to apply.

❓

How many days of free meals can I receive?

The emergency grant covers up to 7 consecutive days completely free of charge. If your financial setback persists past that period, our team will coordinate subsidized plans or bridge extensions.

🛡️

Will my food quality or tiffin packaging change?

Never. You receive the exact same 3-tier food-grade stainless steel dabba containing fresh, steaming hot phulkas, dal tadka, seasonal sabzi, and rice. The food is drawn from the exact same master kitchen batch as all regular subscribers.

🌿

Will other students or hostel guards know?

Zero distinction. The packaging, recipient label, and delivery handling are completely identical to every standard MealMitra order. Neither hostel staff nor roommates will ever be notified of your support status.

❓

What if I need support beyond 7 days?

After 5 days of support, our wellness coordinator sends an informal private message asking if your allowance arrived. If still delayed, we can activate our 50% subsidized plan or discuss a personalized campus repayment deferral.

❓

How do I sponsor a meal or contribute?

Anyone can contribute! You can toggle the "Round Up for Mitra Fund" at weekly checkout, or make a one-time sponsorship of ₹73 (one hot student thali) via UPI directly to the verified campus pool.
